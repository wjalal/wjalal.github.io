Hi, welcome to my portfolio! I'm Wasif Jalal, a PhD student in Computer Science at Arizona State University, advised by [Dr. Asif Salekin](https://asalekin.github.io/) in the [Ubiquitous and Intelligent Sensing (UIS) Lab](https://uislab.github.io/).

My research is at the intersection of deep learning and health. I study trustworthy and fair AI, foundation models for health, and interpretable models for biomedical informatics and bioinformatics. Before ASU, I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET).

Outside research, I enjoy building full-stack web and mobile apps; you can find some of them on my [Projects](/projects/) page.
